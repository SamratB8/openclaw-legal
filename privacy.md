# OpenClaw — Privacy Policy

**Operated by:** Samrat Banerjee, under the name SUnique Homelab  
**Effective date:** 14 October 2026  
**Last updated:** 14 October 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how OpenClaw, an AI-powered personal assistant operated by Samrat Banerjee under the name SUnique Homelab, may process information when you interact with it through Discord or another supported interface.

Our aim is to handle information responsibly, limit unnecessary data collection, and explain the privacy considerations associated with using an AI assistant.

By using OpenClaw, you acknowledge this policy and the data practices applicable to the features you use.

## 2. Information That May Be Processed

Depending on your interactions and the service configuration, OpenClaw may process the following categories of information:

- **Messages and prompts:** Text, questions, commands, and other content you send to the bot.
- **AI responses:** Responses generated by the assistant in connection with your requests.
- **Discord information:** Information made available to the bot through Discord, such as your Discord user ID, username, server or channel context, and message metadata, where applicable.
- **Technical and diagnostic information:** Error reports, timestamps, operational logs, and information necessary to troubleshoot or secure the service.
- **Conversation history and memory:** Information retained to support conversation continuity or features explicitly enabled by the operator.
- **Tool and integration data:** Information required to carry out requests involving configured tools, external services, files, or systems.

The precise information processed depends on the bot's permissions, enabled features, integrations, and configuration.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed for the following purposes:

- Responding to user requests and generating AI-assisted answers.
- Maintaining conversation continuity where supported.
- Executing authorized commands and configured integrations.
- Diagnosing errors and maintaining service reliability.
- Detecting misuse, protecting infrastructure, and enforcing access restrictions.
- Maintaining security and investigating operational incidents.
- Improving the reliability and functionality of the service.

Information will not intentionally be collected for unrelated purposes merely because it is accessible.

## 4. Discord and AI Processing

When you communicate with OpenClaw through Discord, your messages are transmitted through Discord's infrastructure and are subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy and applicable terms.

OpenClaw may use a locally hosted AI model, a third-party AI provider, or a combination of services, depending on its configuration.

When a local model is used, eligible inference may take place on infrastructure controlled by the operator. This does not mean that the entire communication process is local: Discord, external integrations, or other configured providers may still process relevant information.

If a third-party AI provider or integration is used, the information sent to that service will be subject to the provider's applicable terms and privacy practices.

Users should not assume that every request is processed exclusively on the operator's device.

## 5. Storage and Retention

Depending on the configuration, information may be stored in application data, conversation history, databases, logs, backups, or other systems required to operate OpenClaw.

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for its intended operational purpose, including maintaining conversation continuity, troubleshooting issues, preserving security records, or recovering from failures.

Retention periods may vary by information type and system configuration. Some information may remain in backups or third-party systems for a period after it is no longer used in the active service.

OpenClaw does not promise that every message is automatically deleted immediately after a response.

## 6. Sharing and Disclosure

OpenClaw is not intended to sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed or disclosed where necessary to:

- Deliver a requested feature through Discord or another configured provider.
- Operate integrations explicitly enabled by the operator.
- Protect the service, its users, or its infrastructure.
- Respond to valid legal obligations or lawful requests.
- Investigate abuse, security incidents, or suspected unauthorized activity.

The operator does not intend to make private conversations publicly available. However, visibility may depend on Discord channel permissions, bot configuration, enabled logging, and the destination of any requested action.

Users should avoid sending confidential information to public Discord channels.

## 7. Access Permissions and Security

OpenClaw may have access to information and capabilities determined by its Discord permissions and configured integrations.

Access to the bot may be restricted to authorized users or servers. These restrictions depend on the controls actually configured by the operator.

Reasonable technical and organizational measures may be used to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, no internet-connected service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Users should not send passwords, authentication tokens, private keys, financial credentials, or other highly sensitive information through the bot.

## 8. User Choices and Requests

Users should avoid submitting information that is not necessary for their requests.

Where technically supported and applicable, users may request information about the handling of their personal information or ask for deletion of information associated with their interactions.

Such requests may be limited by technical capabilities, security requirements, legitimate operational needs, legal obligations, or information retained by third-party platforms.

Deleting a Discord message does not necessarily delete copies already retained in application logs, conversation history, backups, or third-party systems.

## 9. Children's Privacy

OpenClaw is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Users must comply with applicable age requirements and the terms of the platforms through which they access the service.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, avoid sharing that information further and use an appropriate available reporting or contact channel.

## 10. Third-Party Services and External Links

OpenClaw may rely on Discord, AI model providers, hosting infrastructure, and other third-party services.

Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. The operator does not control all of their data practices, security measures, retention periods, or availability.

Users should review the policies of relevant third-party services when deciding what information to submit.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to OpenClaw, its configuration, integrations, or applicable requirements.

The latest version will be published at the designated Privacy Policy URL, together with its updated date. Where appropriate, material changes will be communicated through the service.

## 12. Contact and Privacy Questions

For privacy-related questions or requests, use an official contact or reporting channel made available by the operator of SUnique Homelab, where available.

Personal contact information is not published in this policy. This does not remove any legal obligations that may apply to the operator.
